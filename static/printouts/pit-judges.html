Aerospace Jam

Pit Judges

Visit teams in the pit, ask consistent questions, and save scores before moving on.

Your Mission

Judge design and pit areas fairly, ask the same core questions, and leave clear notes when something stands out.

You Will Use

Judge Login Judging Pit Scores My Awards
1

Before You Start

  1. Log in.
  2. Confirm your pit judging pages.
  3. Plan your route through the pit.
  4. Charge your device.
2

For Every Team

  1. Find the team.
  2. Open them on your device.
  3. Ask your planned questions.
  4. Enter scores while the conversation is fresh.
3

Before You Walk Away

  1. Add notes if needed.
  2. Save the score.
  3. Then move to the next team.

Keep It Consistent

  • Ask the same core questions.
  • Give teams time to answer.
  • Use the same standard all day.

Never Do This

  • Do not save everything at the end of the day.
  • Do not skip notes when something matters.
  • Do not compare teams out loud.
