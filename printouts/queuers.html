Aerospace Jam

Queuers

Know who is next, go get them, and bring them to the correct arena on time.

Your Mission

Follow the list on your screen, find the next team, and physically move them toward your arena.

You Will Use

Queuer Dashboard Next Team To Grab Upcoming List Arena Number
1

Before The First Team

  1. Log in.
  2. Confirm your arena number.
  3. Look at the next few teams.
2

For Every Team

  1. Check Next Team To Grab.
  2. Go find that team.
  3. Tell them which arena to go to.
  4. Bring them toward the arena.
3

Then Repeat

  1. Return to the dashboard.
  2. Check who is next.
  3. Go get them.
  4. Repeat all day.

Say It Like This

  • "You are next for Arena 2."
  • "Please come with me now."
  • "Stay nearby until the facilitator starts your run."

Never Do This

  • Do not press match timing buttons.
  • Do not change the team order yourself.
  • Do not declare a no-show on your own.
  • Do not stop checking the next team list.
