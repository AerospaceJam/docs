# Congeniality Bonus

Towards the end of the competition, each team will be prompted to submit their nomination for the Congeniality Bonus. The Congeniality Bonus allows teams to award up to 5 points total to up to 5 other teams which they feel are deserving of a bonus, either for following the [Aerospace Jam Ethos](/rules/laws/#the-aerospace-jam-ethos), or being especially friendly or helpful to other teams in helping them troubleshoot or otherwise solve issues that they may experience on competition day.

## Total Possible Distributable Congeniality Bonus: 5 Points (per team)

:::note

A team can hypothetically gain an infinite amount of bonus points from the Congeniality Bonus - but this would require a lot of work to sway the favors of the other teams. Keep this in mind during competition day!

:::

---

## Rules and Procedure

To ensure fairness and clarity, the Congeniality Bonus will be administered according to the following rules:

- **Voting Mechanism:** Each team will be given the opportunity to nominate up to **five** other teams. For each team you nominate, that team will receive **one** bonus point. You cannot give more than one point to a single team.

- **Self-Nomination:** Teams are not permitted to vote for themselves. Any self-nominations will be discarded.

- **Submission:** Nominations will be collected via a form provided on competition day. Only one official submission per team will be accepted, and it must be submitted by the team's adult mentor.

- **Scoring:** The total bonus points awarded to a team will be the sum of all valid nomination points received from other teams. This final bonus will be added to the team's grand total score for the competition.

- **Spirit of the Award:** This bonus is intended to reward genuine acts of sportsmanship and collaboration. Organized vote-trading or other attempts to manipulate the outcome are against the [Aerospace Jam Ethos](/rules/laws/#the-aerospace-jam-ethos) and may result in disqualification from the bonus at the judges' discretion.

- **Finality:** All tallied results for the Congeniality Bonus are final and are not subject to dispute.

:::info Example

If Team A is nominated by Team B, Team C, and Team D, Team A will receive a total of 3 bonus points added to their final competition score.

:::
